# Code of Conduct

PersoAtlas is for learners of every background and level. We want contributing to it to be welcoming for everyone.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be kind, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, give and take feedback gracefully, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's security/contact channel (see SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially.
